Responsible Gambling

A lot of this site jokes about gambling behavior. The jokes stop being funny when gambling stops being entertainment.

Content Position

Balls Deep International covers gambling culture as satire and commentary. It is not a sportsbook, tipster service, or betting advisory product.

If you choose to gamble, do it legally, do it within your means, and assume you can lose the full amount.

Warning Signs

If betting is causing stress, secrecy, debt, relationship damage, or the feeling that you need to chase losses, step back immediately.

Do not gamble to solve financial problems, regulate your mood, or recover money you already lost.

Get Help

United States support is available through 1-800-GAMBLER and state-specific responsible-gaming programs where available.

If the behavior is no longer recreational, get actual help instead of another deposit match.
