The Browns Traded Myles Garrett In June And Blew Up Every Futures Bet I Forgot I Made

There is no football for months and the league is already costing me money

Myles Garrett To The Rams: A June Trade That Detonated Bets I Did Not Remember Placing

June 2, 2026

It is June 2. There is no football. There will not be football that counts for a very long time. The grass is growing, the stadiums are empty, and I, a man of leisure and poor decisions, was finally enjoying a quiet betting offseason. Then the Cleveland Browns traded Myles Garrett, the best defensive player on the planet, to the Los Angeles Rams, and my phone lit up with the specific notification sound that means a futures ticket I forgot I owned just died in its sleep.

For those who touched grass this week: Cleveland sent Garrett, a two-time Defensive Player of the Year fresh off setting the single-season sack record, to the Rams in exchange for edge rusher Jared Verse, a first-round pick in 2027, a second-rounder in 2028, and a third-rounder in 2029. The Rams are going for it. The Browns are going to the future. And I am going through the five stages of grief over bets I made in a haze sometime in March.

Exhibit One: My Browns Defensive Win-Total Flier

Back in the spring, in a moment of what I generously call analysis, I tailed the idea that Cleveland's defense would carry them to a sneaky-high win total because, and I quote my own group chat, "Garrett is a cheat code and the offense just has to be barely functional." I bought the over. I felt smart. I forgot about it. And now the cheat code plays in Los Angeles, the entire premise of my bet has been loaded into a moving truck, and the only thing carrying that win total is a third-year edge rusher and three draft picks that cannot tackle anyone until I have grandchildren.

A futures bet is a promise you make to a past version of yourself who was confident, wrong, and had no idea the roster would be set on fire in June.

Exhibit Two: The Rams Super Bowl Ticket I Now Own By Accident

Here is the cruelest twist. I do not even have a Rams future. I passed on Los Angeles months ago because their number was "boring" and "no value," which is degenerate for "I wanted a longer-shot ticket that made me feel clever." Now the Rams just added the best pass rusher alive and their Super Bowl odds are going to shorten before I finish typing this sentence. The boring bet I skipped is about to look like a genius play, and the interesting bet I made is currently being shipped to Cleveland in a cardboard box with Jared Verse's name on it. I have, once again, found the one path through the woods that ends in a pit.

Exhibit Three: The Verse Believers Are Coming And I Have No Position

And do not get me started on the Browns side. Cleveland just got a genuinely good young edge rusher and a small fortune in picks, which means a certain kind of sicko is about to bet the Browns "defense stays elite, Verse breaks out, rebuild ahead of schedule" angle. That sicko is usually me. But I already blew my Browns position on the Garrett win-total flier, so I get to watch a brand new betting narrative form in real time with absolutely no skin in it, which is its own special torture. It is like getting stood up and then watching your date have a great night with someone funnier.

This is the thing nobody warns you about with offseason betting. You think the danger is the games. The danger is the calendar. The danger is that a front office can, on a random Tuesday in June with the sun out and nothing on the schedule, trade a generational player and quietly euthanize three of your tickets before you have even had lunch. There is no whistle. There is no final score. There is just a push notification and the slow realization that the league found a way to beat you in a month with no football in it.

So congratulations to the Rams, condolences to me, and a respectful nod to the Browns for getting a haul. I will be reassessing my entire futures portfolio, by which I mean I will be making three new bad bets to replace the three that just died. For more reasons to never do this, wander into the Gaped ward or read the rest of the wreckage on Degeneracy. The season is months away. My bankroll is already filing a complaint.
