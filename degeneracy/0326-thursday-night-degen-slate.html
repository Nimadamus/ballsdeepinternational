Thursday Night: 3 NBA Games, 13 NHL Games, and One Bankroll Begging for Mercy

March 26, 2026 • Degeneracy Division • Reading time: however long it takes your parlay to die

Listen. I know what you're thinking. Three NBA games? That's it? That's the slate? What is this, a Tuesday in July? But here's the thing about a three-game NBA slate paired with a 13-game NHL monster: the books are begging you to spread yourself thin. They want you hopping between basketball and hockey like a squirrel on espresso, building seven-leg parlays that connect the Knicks moneyline to the Oilers puck line to the over in Pelicans-Pistons like some kind of degenerate spider web.

And you know what? We're going to do it anyway. Because it's Thursday night, the rent check cleared three days ago, and responsible financial planning is for people who don't have access to a sportsbook at 2 AM.

The NBA: Three Games, Infinite Bad Decisions

New York Knicks at Charlotte Hornets (7:00 PM ET, NBATV)

Line: Hornets -1.5 • O/U: 222.5 • Knicks ML: ~+105

The Knicks roll into Charlotte riding a seven-game winning streak, which is the kind of stat that makes every degen in America want to hammer New York and ride the wave. But Charlotte has won four straight and six of their last seven, including a 44-point demolition of the Kings on Tuesday. Forty-four points. That's not a basketball game, that's a war crime.

So naturally this line is basically a pick 'em. Charlotte -1.5 at home feels like the kind of trap that exists purely to separate you from your money regardless of which side you take. You pick the Knicks because of the streak, they lose by 3. You pick the Hornets because they're home, the Knicks cover by 8. This game was designed in a laboratory by the sportsbooks to cause maximum suffering.

New Orleans Pelicans at Detroit Pistons (7:00 PM ET)

Line: Pistons -4.5 • O/U: 225.5 • Pistons ML: -210

Detroit is laying 4.5 at home against New Orleans, which, if you told me five years ago that the Pistons would be giving nearly five points to anyone, I would have assumed you were having a stroke. But here we are in 2026 and the Pistons are chasing the 1-seed in the East like it's the last slice of pizza at a bachelor party. The total sitting at 225.5 is getting some action on the over. Both these teams can score, and the Pelicans defense is more of a suggestion than an actual system.

Sacramento Kings at Orlando Magic (7:00 PM ET)

Line: Magic -15.5 • O/U: 229.5 • Magic ML: -1250

Fifteen and a half points. Negative twelve-fifty on the moneyline. The Magic are laying more points than most NFL favorites. Sacramento is basically showing up as a Make-A-Wish opponent at this point. If you're betting the Kings moneyline at +825, you're either an absolute psychopath or you know something the rest of us don't, and honestly, in the degeneracy community we respect both equally. The over at 229.5 is probably the only remotely sane play here, because Orlando could score 130 by themselves and still need Sacramento to accidentally stumble into 100.

"The difference between a degenerate and a sharp is that the sharp loses money more slowly."

The NHL: 13 Games and the Death of Your Bankroll

While the NBA is giving us a pathetic three-game appetizer, the NHL decided to throw the entire freezer at us. Thirteen games. That's 26 teams on the ice, which means there are roughly 4.7 billion possible parlay combinations, and every single one of them will lose by one goal in overtime. Hockey parlays don't die gracefully. They get taken behind the shed.

The Main Event: Oilers at Golden Knights (9:30 PM ET, ESPN)

Line: Golden Knights -1.5 (+172) • O/U: 6.5 • Vegas ML: -137 • Edmonton ML: +114

This is the one. The Pacific Division blood feud. Edmonton (35-28-9) rolling into T-Mobile Arena to face Vegas (32-26-14) with both teams scrapping for playoff positioning like two raccoons fighting over a dumpster. The over/under at 6.5 is interesting because these two teams treat defense like an optional side quest. Mitch Marner in a Golden Knights jersey still doesn't look right, by the way. He looks like someone Photoshopped a Maple Leaf onto a slot machine.

Edmonton at +114 on the road is tempting, because Connor McDavid in a must-win stretch is basically a cheat code. But Vegas at home under the lights, on ESPN, with the pregame show featuring a guy in a medieval knight costume riding out on a horse? The Golden Knights have an unfair home-ice advantage simply because the visiting team is jet-lagged and hungover from walking through the casino lobby.

Devils at Predators (8:00 PM ET)

Line: Nashville -115 / New Jersey -105 • O/U: 5.5

Another pick 'em, another opportunity to flip a coin and pretend we did research. The under at 5.5 feels like the move here since both these teams can play stingy hockey when they want to, but I've said "the under feels like the move" approximately 300 times this season and it's hit maybe 40% of the time. Which, mathematically, means I should probably stop saying it. But I won't. Because growth is for people who don't bet hockey unders.
